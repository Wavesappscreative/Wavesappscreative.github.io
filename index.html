Privacy policy

This policy describes the current Android app, Your Ghost Is the Enemy (also called Ghost Enemy), and this support website. The developer can be contacted at drivfiappscreative@gmail.com. The app is intended for teens and adults aged 13 and older.

Game information on your device

The game stores your best score on your device. Movement recordings used to create replay ghosts are held during a run; the game does not upload those recordings to a developer-operated server. No player account, public profile, chat, or cloud leaderboard is included in this version.

The app does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location. The advertising SDK processes additional information described below.

Advertising and consent

The app integrates Google AdMob for interstitial ads between runs and optional rewarded ads for an extra life. Test builds use demonstration ad units. Google’s User Messaging Platform is used to request applicable privacy choices before ads are requested.

Google states that its Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares IP addresses (which can indicate general location), app and ad interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers, including the advertising ID where available. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. See Google’s Mobile Ads data disclosure for details.

Advertising may be personalized, non-personalized, or limited depending on your choices, location, age-related requirements, and the final ad configuration. Non-personalized ads do not necessarily mean that no data is processed. No separate developer analytics or crash-reporting service is currently added to the app.

Your choices

Where available, use Ad privacy choices in the app to review or change your selections. Android also provides advertising-ID controls in device settings. Choosing not to watch an optional rewarded ad does not prevent ordinary gameplay.

You can clear the app’s local data through Android settings or uninstall the app to remove its local best score. Doing so does not automatically delete information already processed by Google. Contact us for questions or requests about information you have provided directly to us.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond, troubleshoot, and handle privacy requests. Our email provider processes these messages to deliver the service. Please send only information needed for your request.

This website

This website has no account registration, advertising scripts, tracking pixels, or analytics added by the developer. The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and browser details, to serve and protect the website. Selecting an email link opens your email application; the site does not collect form submissions.

Retention and security

Your best score remains on your device until the app’s data is cleared or removed. Support correspondence should be retained only as long as reasonably needed to address the request and satisfy applicable legal requirements. Google controls retention of information it processes under its own policies; this developer cannot promise a fixed deletion period for Google’s systems.

We aim to limit the information needed for support and restrict its use to the purposes described here. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children and teenagers

This app is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can investigate and delete information we control where appropriate. Applicable protections for teenagers must also be reflected in the release’s advertising configuration.

Privacy requests and policy changes

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain personal information, withdraw consent, or object to certain processing. Send requests to our support email. We may need to verify your request and will respond as required by applicable law. Requests about data controlled by Google may need to be directed to Google.

We will update this page if the app’s practices change and revise the date shown. This draft must be checked against the final release, launch regions, and service-provider settings before it becomes the published policy.
